# Contributing

The goal is to help learners follow a complete explanation without hidden prerequisites or inaccurate shortcuts.

## Share a learning experience

Open an issue with:

- The topic and your starting knowledge.
- The AI tool and model, if known.
- Your exact starting prompt and the skill version.
- A short response excerpt where the explanation helped or became confusing.
- What you expected the explanation to make clear.

Remove private information before sharing a chat or screenshot. Please describe your experience rather than generalizing it to every learner or model.

## Suggest a change

Explain the specific teaching problem the change solves. Keep the instructions self-contained and broadly useful across subjects. Preserve the causal progression, precise mechanisms, and small technical examples. Avoid adding unrelated workflows or duplicating instructions.

For a substantial change, try a fresh conversation on a different topic to check that it still works outside the example that motivated it. Include actual output excerpts and clearly label any edits.

## Keep the download current

When changing the skill, update the version notes and rebuild the skill ZIP so the downloadable instructions match `skills/teach-from-zero/SKILL.md`. The ZIP should contain one `teach-from-zero` directory with the skill files and a copy of `LICENSE`. Do not package private chats, local account metadata, or editor caches.

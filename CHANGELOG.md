# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-19

Initial public distribution package.

- Self-contained instructions for causal, experience-first explanations.
- Guidance for prerequisites, analogies, code, maths, pacing, and confusion recovery.
- Worked networking, Kotlin, and mathematical examples.
- Chat-upload instructions, example prompts, and a skill ZIP.

The teaching instructions are the existing personally tested version. This release packages them for sharing; it does not introduce a new teaching method or claim broad comparative testing.

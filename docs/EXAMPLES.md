# Starting prompts

Upload the skill or supply its full instructions first. Replace these starting points with your own topic and experience.

## Completely new to a topic

> Use this skill to teach me fermentation from scratch. I’m completely blank—I don’t know anything about it. Start with something I can picture in a kitchen.

## Some everyday knowledge

> Use this skill to explain why bread dough rises. I know yeast is an ingredient, but I don’t know what it does. Explain how we get from a bowl of dough to bubbles trapped inside it.

## An unfamiliar creative field

> Use this skill to teach me musical harmony from zero. I listen to music, but I don’t know what a note, interval, or chord actually means. Help me understand what I would hear as we introduce each idea.

## A gap inside a subject you know

> Use this skill to explain callbacks in Kotlin. I understand variables and ordinary functions, but I get confused about passing a function and who eventually calls it. Build that connection slowly, then show a small example.

## Maths without skipped operations

> Use this skill to explain solving a basic equation. I can add and subtract, but I don’t understand why signs seem to change when numbers move across the equals sign. Show what operation is actually happening.

## When a lesson loses you

> I followed until you introduced [concept]. I don’t understand what it is or how it causes the next step. Use the same example, return to that point, and rebuild that connection.

## Going deeper after the first explanation

> I can now picture the basic process. Use the same teaching style to explain [specific mechanism] more precisely. Tell me where the earlier approximation stops being accurate.

You do not need a perfect prompt. Start honestly with what you know and where you get lost. A focused learning goal helps the AI choose how much to cover in one answer.
